# 07 — Cost-per-Mile Benchmarking Brief (for Aug 6 10am)

**Purpose:** truth-test DTE's claim that HWC's awarded price is "above industry baseline."
**Bottom line up front:** there is no single "industry baseline" for this work — published
figures span $30k to $6M per mile. The most relevant benchmark is **DTE's own audited 4.8kV
conversion program expectation of ~$1.25–1.56M per mile** — and HWC's post-UG price (~$1.34M/mi
on the 11.5-mile proposal basis) sits INSIDE that range. The $2M demand implies ~$1.16M/mi,
BELOW DTE's own audited floor. DTE's new internal target, not HWC's price, is the outlier.

## Published Benchmarks

| Benchmark | $/mile | What it includes | Why it does/doesn't compare |
|-----------|--------|------------------|------------------------------|
| Rural co-op greenfield 3-phase extension | **$30k–110k** | Bare new line on clear ROW, co-op labor, no cutover, minimal engineering | NOT comparable — no conversion, no live-system cutover, no EPC scope. If DTE's comp set looks like this, it's apples-to-oranges |
| OH distribution rebuild / hardening w/ larger conductor | **~$300k/circuit mile** | Construction-only reconductor-class rebuild | Closest to a "construction-only" comp; excludes engineering, procurement, PM, cutover complexity |
| EEI overhead transmission replacement (minimums) | **$174k (rural)–$377k (urban)** | Transmission, minimum replacement | Context only — different asset class |
| **DTE's OWN 4.8kV conversion program (MPSC-ordered 3rd-party audit)** | **~$1.25M–1.56M** | $20–25B to convert remaining 16,000 OH miles of 4.8kV by 2040 — full program cost | **The most on-point comp**: same utility, same program type, DTE's own planning number |
| Undergrounding (CA/FL ranges) | $1.85M–6M+ | OH→UG conversion | Context for why the deleted UG scope was expensive |

## HWC Implied $/mile — fill in at the meeting

- If predominantly 3-phase + neutral (4 wires): ≈ **6.6 line-miles**
- If mixed 3-phase/1-phase (~3.5 wires avg): ≈ **7.6 line-miles**

**Formula for the meeting:** (contract value post-UG-removal) ÷ (total program line-miles) = implied $/mile.
Compare that to $1.25–1.56M (DTE's own program number) and ~$300k (construction-only rebuild comp).

## Questions That Dismantle the Claim (ask DTE in writing)
1. What is the $/mile target, and what is in the numerator? (Engineering? Procurement? PM?
   Contingency? Restoration? Traffic control? Material — and who furnishes it?)
2. What comp set produced "consistently delivering similar builds at lower cost per mile" —
   which projects, conversion or new-build, what geography, union/non-union, storm vs planned?
3. Does the comp include live-system cutover and phase conversion (4.8→8.3kV), or greenfield?
4. How does the target reconcile with the MPSC audit's $20–25B / 16,000-mile program figure?
5. Is DTE-furnished job stock priced into the comps the same way as in our contract?

## Talking Points
- "Industry baseline" spans two orders of magnitude in public sources; a claim of "above
  baseline" is meaningless without the comp definition. We welcome the comparison — send the math.
- Against DTE's own audited program planning number ($1.25–1.56M/mi), our post-UG price
  (~$1.34M/mi) sits INSIDE their range — and their $2M demand would push us BELOW their own
  audited floor. We are not the outlier; the new target is.
- The one legitimately cheaper comp (co-op greenfield at $30–110k/mi) isn't this scope: no
  conversion, no cutover, no EPC services, no DTE standards/restoration burden.
- Where a real gap may exist: standalone-WO pricing vs program pricing — which is exactly what
  our sequencing proposal addresses (see 06 / sequencing model). We fix that WITH them, not by
  discounting into a benchmark nobody has defined.
- They awarded this price. The estimate didn't change; their target did. Partnership framing:
  bring the target and its math to the workshop and we'll engineer to it.

## Caveats
- Line-mile estimates derive from conductor quantities (15 of ~24 OH CWOs) — replace with actual
  program route miles from design.
- The $300k/mi rebuild comp is an older published figure; escalate ~30-50% to 2026 dollars.
- We do not yet know DTE's comp set; these are published/public figures, not their internal ones.
