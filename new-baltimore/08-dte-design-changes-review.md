## What DTE is proposing

**Scope changes by highlighted section:**
1. **Green section (#1, #2 — the long SW corridor):**
   - #1: change "Reconductor 3PH 1/0 ACSR" → "Reconductor to 3PH **336** ACSR" — a conductor
     **UPGRADE** (cost ADD: heavier wire, potentially heavier poles/framing/guying).
   - #2: change "Reconductor 3PH lead with 636 AA **armless** construction with shield wire" →
     "Reconductor 4.8kV 3PH lead with **33[6?] ACSR** using **flat-arm** construction, replace
     poles **only as necessary**, use **existing neutral from existing 13.2kV**" — a major
     **DOWNGRADE/descope** (deletes premium armless construction, shield wire, and most pole
     replacements on that lead). NOTE: drawing text says "33 ACSR" — presumed typo for 336;
     get written confirmation, do not assume.
2. **Blue sections:** replace **copper conductor only** with 1/0 ACSR; keep existing phase
   count; no other reconductoring. (Descope relative to full rebuild.)
3. **Red sections:** **NO reconductoring required.** Install neutral only where using
   secondary as neutral is not possible. (Largest descope — several NE sections: Main St,
   Walpole/Murdick, Green St areas.)

**Design notes (1–5):** pole replacement by condition/rating only, ridge-pin construction
preferred on old poles; no blanket secondary reconductoring; eliminate #6/#4 copper on 4.8kV
leads (replace w/ 1/0 ACSR, 336 ACSR, or 636 AA "based on engineering"); non-reconductored
1PH/3PH sections designed so second phase becomes neutral at conversion (ex: LS Murdick Dr E
of Walpole St); non-reconductored 3PH sections designed with neutral connected.

**Callout:** "BRING TO ROW — COORDINATE WITH TRK4911 PROJECT FOR LEAD ALONG COUNTY LINE ROAD"
— confirms the TRK-4911 interface (workstream 03) now sits inside the changed scope.

## Strategic read — this validates our position

DTE is answering their own $2M demand with **scope changes**, not a demand that we reprice
retained work. This is exactly the path the C-suite posture allows (06 negotiating rules):
**scope reduction at bid values through the change process.** It also confirms the diagnostic
from 06: the driver is BUDGET — they are descoping to hit a number, not defending a benchmark.
Posture response: engage constructively and fast on THEIR mechanism, concede nothing on price.

## Commercial guardrails (non-negotiable)

1. **A preliminary planning drawing is not direction.** Zein Hassan / Regional Planning has no
   contract-change authority — only John Chang does. Acknowledge receipt in writing, log as a
   proposed change, request formal issuance through the change process. Protect the 10-day
   notice windows: written acknowledgment dated on receipt.
2. **Price the deltas BOTH ways at bid values.** Credits for deleted work (red sections, #2
   downgrade, pole replacements avoided) AND adds for new/upgraded work (#1 conductor upgrade,
   neutral installs in red sections, second-phase-to-neutral conversion work, any framing
   changes). No cherry-picking credits.
3. **Stranded material is compensable.** POs placed Jun/Jul 2025 against the OLD design.
   636 AA, armless framing hardware, and shield wire likely on order or delivered ($953k
   committed / $687k invoiced at PLS as of 7/28). Change creates new surplus (return/restock
   costs) and new demand (336 ACSR — check lead times). Feed the surplus triage workbook.
4. **Engineering rework is a cost.** Completed/in-progress designs for affected CWOs must be
   redone; that re-engineering is part of the change price, at bid rates.
5. **Pole-quantity risk must be defined.** "Replace poles only as necessary / by condition"
   converts a fixed pole count (197 + 70 concrete bid basis) into a condition-driven quantity.
   Require written condition criteria + unit-rate add/delete treatment, or we own the risk of
   every pole that fails at stringing.
6. **Engineering acceptance is ours.** Reusing the existing 13.2kV neutral and converting a
   phase to neutral are DTE design choices with loading/clearance/grounding implications — CVR
   (EOR) reviews and accepts or takes exception in writing. If it doesn't check out, the fix
   is priced work.

## Impact on the $/mile fight (07)

"pole/neutral-only mileage" nuance Hayley flagged. If DTE later re-runs $/mile on the reduced
scope, both numerator AND denominator shrink — insist any future benchmark discussion uses
matched scope and mileage. TRK-4911 miles remain excluded (07 rule stands).
