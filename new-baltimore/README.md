# New Baltimore Project

Project workspace for all New Baltimore tracking and deliverables.

## Key Facts

- **Scope basis:** 39 work orders (estimate/plan spread across them)
- **Construction start:** Next spring (2027)
- **Client:** DTE — we owe them an "open book" cost breakdown
- **Schedule prerequisite:** TRK-4911, being design/built by Burns & McDonnell (external — track, don't control)
- **Easements budget:** $200k (source of that number unverified — see workstream 01)
- **Procurement note:** 8 of the underground purchase orders will be cancelled

## Sequencing Logic

1. **02 (Estimate/GP analysis)** feeds **05 (DTE open book)** — you can't hand DTE a credible cost breakdown until you know where actuals stand against the estimate.
2. **02** also feeds **04 (Master Schedule)** — cost/resource loading needs the reconciled estimate, including the 8 cancelled underground POs.
3. **03 (TRK-4911)** constrains **04** — the master schedule must carry TRK-4911 milestones as predecessors wherever it makes sense.
4. **01 (Easements)** is independent but should close before the open book goes to DTE, since the $200k line will be in it.
